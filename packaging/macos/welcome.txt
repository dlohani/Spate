Cascade is a download manager that takes over downloads from your browser: parallel connections, resume, a queue, and a menu-bar app that starts at login.

This installs for the current user only (no administrator password):
  - the Cascade app and its browser helper under ~/Library/Application Support/cascade
  - browser integration for Chrome, Chromium, Brave, Edge and Vivaldi
  - a login item so Cascade is always available (menu-bar icon)

After installing, one manual step remains in the browser: load the Cascade extension (the final screen shows where it is).
