Spate is installed and running in your menu bar (also in Spotlight and Launchpad as "Spate").

Browser integration: the installer registered Spate with the Chromium-family browsers on this Mac (Chrome, Chromium, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi).
  - If the Spate extension is published in the browser's store, restart the browser: it installs the extension by itself and asks once to enable it.
  - Otherwise load it once by hand: chrome://extensions -> "Developer mode" -> "Load unpacked" -> ~/Library/Application Support/spate/extension

Then download anything. Spate asks once how to handle downloads and remembers your answer.

If the extension's Options page shows a different Extension ID than expected, run:
  ~/Library/Application Support/spate/bin/register-extension <ID>

To uninstall: ~/Library/Application Support/spate/bin/uninstall
