Cascade is installed and running in your menu bar.

Last step, in your browser (Chrome, Chromium, Brave, Edge or Vivaldi):
  1. Open chrome://extensions and turn on "Developer mode".
  2. Click "Load unpacked" and choose:
     ~/Library/Application Support/cascade/extension
  3. Download anything. Cascade asks once how to handle downloads and remembers your answer.

If the extension's Options page shows a different Extension ID than expected, re-run:
  ~/Library/Application Support/cascade/bin/register-extension <ID>

To uninstall: run ~/Library/Application Support/cascade/bin/uninstall
